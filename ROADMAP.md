# Roadmap

No dates or community demand are claimed.

1. Full-project source preview: confirm ownership/license and private reporting; prepare a reviewed allowlisted application candidate and honest feature matrix. Keep parser/scoring available as an optional standalone extraction.
2. Core quality: bounded DOCX decompression, malformed answer validation, table/formula fixtures, complete grading inputs and score invariants, versioned APIs and regression tests.
3. Application UX and independence: resolve submission/timeout/unanswered grading, accessible forms/dialogs, draft recovery and capability-state issues in docs/ux/AUDIT.md; choose Firebase or MongoDB as authoritative path, align authentication and remove unused legacy surfaces; implement teacher/tenant authorization and emulator/integration tests.
4. Operator readiness: reproducible clean install, end-to-end demo, deployment/backup/restore guide, student-data retention, accessibility and internationalization review.
5. Maintenance: assign named maintainer and realistic review cadence, triage real issues, record releases and verified educational feedback. Do not manufacture stars or usage.
